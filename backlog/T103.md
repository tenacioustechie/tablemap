## Goal
During play the DM opens and closes doors by clicking them on the canvas, whatever tab is open; the Walls panel lists doors with their state. Plan: `docs/plans/2026-09-v2-vision-and-depth.md`.

## Design
- Convenience op `map/door-set-open { id, segmentId, open }` (reducer: no-op unless the segment is a door). Guard + `opsForPlayer` pass-through.
- DM canvas: doors are always clickable (their Konva line gets `listening`, hit stroke width 12 screen px), even while a fog or draw tool is armed, in the same way note markers stay clickable (T011); a click toggles `open`. A door's look: closed = solid short bar in the door colour, open = the bar swung 45° as a thin outline. Cursor `pointer` on hover.
- Walls panel: a **Doors** list (`door-item`, label "Door n · open/closed", toggle button `door-toggle`), clicking an item selects and centres the canvas on it.
- A hook for later tasks: `store.onOps` listeners can react to a door change (T106 re-runs vision). Nothing else here.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Clicking a closed door on the DM canvas opens it, with the brush armed or not; clicking again closes it
- [ ] Door state syncs to the player mirror and persists through reload and export/import
- [ ] The panel list and the canvas agree at all times

## Test requirements
- Unit: `shared.test.ts` (op; wall id → no-op), `dm/WallPanel.test.tsx` (list + toggle), `dm/DmView.test.tsx` (door click while a tool is armed does not paint).
- E2E: extend `e2e/walls.spec.ts` — click the door, `data-walls` shows `open`, the player mirror (a `data-doors-open` count on the player root) follows; reload keeps it.
- Docs: user guide §4 "Doors during play", architecture, ADR 0024 amended (doors are always clickable).
