## Goal
DM view shows the player viewport as a draggable rectangle (size derived from calibration + TV resolution); dragging it pans the player view. Rotate player view in 90-degree steps. Blackout button (instant black). Splash screen (campaign title card / chosen image) shown on scene switch or on demand.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Player pan follows the DM viewport rectangle smoothly
- [ ] Blackout and splash override all layers instantly
- [ ] Rotation preserves calibration scale

## Test requirements
- Unit: viewport rect <-> player transform math incl. rotation. E2E: blackout turns player page black; splash shows.
