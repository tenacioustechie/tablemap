## Goal
Square grid layer. Alignment mode: DM clicks/drags two opposite corners of one printed map square; app derives cellSize + offset. Grid toggle, opacity and colour, independently for DM and player views.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Alignment on a fixture map with a known grid yields correct cellSize/offset (+-1 px)
- [ ] Grid settings persist and sync to player

## Test requirements
