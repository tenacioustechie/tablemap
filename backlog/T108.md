## Goal
A **Fill** fog tool: click a grid cell and every cell reachable from it without crossing a wall or closed door is revealed (or hidden, in hide mode) as one op. Plan: `docs/plans/2026-09-v2-vision-and-depth.md` §2.

## Design
- Pure `map/roomFill.ts`: `roomCells(startCell, grid, sceneSize, segments, limit): number[] | "too-large"` — 4-neighbour flood over grid cells; a step between two cells is blocked when any blocking segment (T104 `blockingSegments`) intersects the shared cell edge (segment/segment intersection, inclusive of touching); the map border bounds the fill; `limit` (default 20 000 cells) aborts with `"too-large"`. Output is the flat `[x, y, w, h, …]` rects list of the T008 `rects` shape (one rect per cell, or merged horizontal runs — merge is a nice-to-have, assert equivalence if done).
- Fog tab: **Fill** joins brush / rectangle / lasso (`fog-tool-fill`), obeying the reveal/hide mode; snapping is implied (cells). Hover previews the room (recomputed on cell change, not per pixel); click commits one `map/fog-op` with the rects shape (feather off: it is cell-snapped). "too-large" shows a warning in the panel and commits nothing.
- Shortcut `F` (T018 keymap); tool status chip label "Fog · fill".
- Works with no walls at all (fills the whole map, capped by the limit → warning), so the tool is useful before any wall is drawn only on small maps; the guide says draw walls first.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Click inside a closed room: exactly its cells are revealed; the door cell's far side is not; open the door and fill again: both rooms
- [ ] A diagonal wall crossing cells blocks along its whole length (no leak through corners)
- [ ] Fill is one undo step; the player mirror applies the same rects
- [ ] Oversized fill warns and changes nothing
