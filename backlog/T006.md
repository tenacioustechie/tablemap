## Goal
Calibration screen on the player window: on-screen target square + controls on DM side to grow/shrink it until it measures 1 inch (or 25 mm) with a ruler; alternatively enter TV diagonal + resolution. Derives pxPerInch, stored per display (screen id/size heuristic). Player stage zoom locks to calibration; DM has an explicit "overview" override toggle that visibly un-calibrates.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] With calibration set, player-view cell pixel size = pxPerInch * cellInches regardless of map image resolution
- [ ] Calibration survives reload and applies when the player window reopens on the same display

## Test requirements
- Unit: pxPerInch derivation (diagonal+resolution and measured-square paths); locked-scale computation.
