## Goal
Fog mask (offscreen canvas, map-sized, black). Reveal ops drawn destination-out. Store holds the op log + current mask; ops sync to player as operations (never bitmaps). DM renders mask at 50% alpha, player at 100%. New maps fully fogged. Hide-all / reveal-all. Mask persisted as PNG blob with the map.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Ops applied on player produce a pixel-identical mask to DM (same op log)
- [ ] Fog state survives reload and scene switches
- [ ] Reveal visible on player page <100 ms after DM action (e2e timestamp check, generous CI threshold)

## Test requirements
- Unit: op application math on a small canvas (node-canvas-free: use OffscreenCanvas in jsdom or abstract raster ops to a testable module). E2E: reveal rectangle on DM page, screenshot region of player page shows map pixels; blackout-by-default before reveal.
